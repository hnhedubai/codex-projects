# Oman Data-Centre Land Tracker Glossary

## Allocation

A signed land allocation, usufruct, lease, or equivalent land right. It is an
outcome of the tracker workflow, not a synonym for a shortlisted parcel.

## Contact

A publicly sourced professional person or communication channel linked to an
organisation. An email must retain its Reoon verification result, including
when the contact is rejected.

## Evidence

A source-backed fact, document, map, letter, or verification result used to
support an assessment. Only official authority evidence and signed
utility/operator confirmation can satisfy a hard gate.

## Hard Gate

A mandatory requirement which must be evidenced before a parcel can be marked
allocation-ready. A score can rank parcels but cannot clear a hard gate.

## JEV Review

An independent contact-rejection validation performed after the tracker has
automatically rejected a contact. The contact remains pending review until JEV
records its outcome and evidence; the outcome either confirms the rejection or
reactivates the contact.

## JEV Vote

An append-only advisory decision made by JEV at a named review checkpoint. It
records the subject, input snapshot, outcome, confidence, reasons, and review
time. It never clears a hard gate, approves allocation, sends communication,
or replaces a human Approver.

## Organisation

A company, government department, operator, landowner, utility, carrier,
advisor, or other stakeholder that can affect a land opportunity.

## Parcel

A candidate physical land area assessed for a data-centre development. It has
map geometry or a location reference, an IT-load target of at least 10 MW, and
separate capacity evidence for connected, firm, backup, and expandable power.

## Research Project

A reusable country or market research container. The initial project is Oman;
future countries use the same model rather than a separate tracker.

## Rejection

A preserved decision not to pursue a parcel, organisation, or contact, with a
reason, supporting evidence, reviewer, and possible reactivation state.

## Research Run

A bounded retrieval activity that records its tool, query, researcher,
timestamp, scope, and output. Exa supports discovery; Firecrawl is limited to
one official-source validation request per run.
