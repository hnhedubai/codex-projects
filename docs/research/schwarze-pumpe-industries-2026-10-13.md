# Industriepark Schwarze Pumpe: industry screening

Research date: 2026-10-13

## Scope

The application now exposes named site occupants when `Industriepark Schwarze Pumpe` is selected in the German industrial-zone dropdown. This is an industrial-site discovery list, not proof of hydrogen demand. The park operator describes the site as having more than 125 companies and about 5,500 employees (the operator's cited baseline is June 2020), so a public page cannot be treated as a complete current tenant register.

## Caveat

The table is intentionally conservative. It identifies industrial leads and exclusion categories; it does not assert annual fuel consumption, hydrogen purity, pressure, delivery form, or tonnes-per-day demand. Those fields require plant-specific evidence from official company, permit, EU ETS, government, or other credible sources before classification as a confirmed consumer.
