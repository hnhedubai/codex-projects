# Dominik opportunities — information required before proceeding

## Scope

Dominik's message describes two immediate project tracks:

1. An Abu Dhabi land-and-power opportunity for a data centre and/or crypto-mining project.
2. An Oman industrial-zone opportunity for a comparable project.

Hydrogen offtakers are a separate origination workstream and should not be mixed into either data-centre proposal until there is a named offtaker and a defined product specification.

## Preliminary assessment

The Abu Dhabi proposal is not yet bankable evidence of land, permits, power, or government support. The words “owns land”, “has permits”, “strong connections”, and “can arrange licences” need documentary verification. Abu Dhabi requires the proposed economic activity, legal form, premises and relevant permits to be identified through the ADDED/TAMM licensing pathway. A virtual-asset activity may also trigger UAE federal and local licensing requirements; data-centre operation and crypto mining should therefore be treated as separate regulatory cases until counsel confirms otherwise.

Oman is potentially more structured for an industrial/data-centre project because OPAZ supervises special economic zones, free zones and industrial cities, and the official framework provides a route involving application, land allocation and a usufruct agreement. Electricity pricing is not safely represented by a single informal “per-kWh” figure: large commercial and industrial users can fall under cost-reflective tariffs, which include energy, transmission, distribution and supply components. A site-specific utility quotation is required.

## Information Dominik must provide — Abu Dhabi

### Land and rights

- Exact plot location, coordinates, plot number and total area.
- Legal owner and a current title deed or equivalent ownership evidence.
- Whether the land is owned personally, by a company, or held under lease/usufruct.
- Copy of every permit already issued, issuing authority, activity covered, expiry date and conditions.
- Zoning designation and written confirmation that data-centre, high-load computing and/or crypto-mining use is permitted.
- Encumbrances, mortgages, third-party rights, access rights and any restrictions on subleasing or joint ventures.

### Electricity

- Current utility connection point and utility account/customer reference.
- Approved connected capacity, firm/contracted capacity, available spare capacity and expansion capacity.
- Voltage level, substation, feeder and distance to the plot.
- Written utility confirmation of continuous capacity for the proposed IT load, not merely a verbal indication.
- Tariff structure, demand charges, connection/upgrade costs, deposits, wheeling or network charges, and expected energisation date.
- Power-quality, redundancy and outage information; proposed N, N+1 or 2N design.
- Whether renewable power is physical, contractual, or only an offset/attribute claim.

### Project definition

- Intended IT load in MW, initial and ultimate phases, annual operating hours and PUE target.
- Whether the project is a conventional data centre, AI/HPC facility, crypto-mining facility, or a mixed-use campus.
- Mining equipment type, expected electrical load per rack, cooling method, noise/heat profile and operating model.
- Target customers or offtakers for data-centre capacity, if any.
- CAPEX budget, operating budget, funding source, investor status and target financial close.
- Proposed ownership percentages, governance, management roles, land contribution, power contribution and cash contributions.
- Required telecom carriers, fibre routes, latency targets, carrier-neutral requirements and physical security expectations.

### Regulatory and commercial proof

- Written legal opinion on whether the intended crypto activity is mining, proprietary operation, virtual-asset services, hosting, or another regulated activity.
- Proposed Abu Dhabi legal entity and exact licensed activities.
- Environmental, building, civil-defence, planning, telecom and electrical approvals required and status of each.
- Draft term sheet for the JV, including exclusivity, milestones, failure rights, dispute resolution and ownership of improvements.
- Independent valuation of the land and any claimed permits.

## Information Dominik must provide — Oman

### Site and zone

- Preferred zone(s): Duqm, Sohar, Salalah, Khazaen, Madayn or another named location.
- Exact parcel, area, coordinates, zoning and the responsible zone operator.
- Proposed tenure: lease, usufruct, allocation or purchase; term, rent, escalation and renewal rights.
- Written confirmation that data-centre, high-load computing and/or crypto-mining activity is accepted in the selected zone.
- Land allocation or usufruct application status and the documents already submitted.

### Utilities and connectivity

- Utility operator and written quotation for the required MW load.
- Connection voltage, firm capacity, redundancy, connection timetable and upgrade cost.
- Full tariff schedule: energy, transmission, distribution, supply, demand, seasonal and other charges.
- Water source, cooling-water availability, discharge requirements and connection cost.
- Fibre providers, route diversity, latency, carrier availability and service-level commitments.
- Road access, logistics, customs status and distance to the nearest port or airport.

### Licensing, tax and compliance

- Exact Omani entity structure and proposed foreign-ownership model.
- Commercial/industrial/technology licence category and activity codes.
- Environmental permit category and whether an EMP or ESIA is required.
- Building, planning, civil-defence, telecom, labour and cybersecurity approvals.
- Tax, customs, VAT, Omanisation and data-residency implications confirmed by Omani advisers.
- Confirmation whether crypto mining is permitted at the chosen site and under what licence or restrictions.

### Bankability

- Site-specific feasibility study, load profile, CAPEX/OPEX model and financing plan.
- Identified technology/operator partner and proof of operating track record.
- Customer contracts or credible demand evidence for colocation, cloud, HPC or other data-centre services.
- Draft land/utility term sheets and a milestone schedule to financial close.

## Hydrogen offtaker workstream

Before approaching potential offtakers, Dominik should provide:

- Hydrogen type and specification: renewable hydrogen, derivatives, ammonia, methanol, e-fuels or another product.
- Target annual volume, delivery pressure/temperature, purity and delivery form.
- Expected start date, contract term, ramp-up profile and required certification.
- Delivery geography and preferred Incoterm or delivery point.
- Indicative price basis and whether a binding offtake, MOU, heads of terms or only introductions are sought.
- Production location assumptions, feedstock, renewable-power basis, water source and carbon accounting approach.
- Project sponsor, funding status, permitting status and technical partner.

## Recommended next step

Do not introduce investors or commit to partners yet. Request the documents above in a controlled data room, verify land ownership and utility capacity independently, obtain written licensing feedback, and prepare separate Abu Dhabi and Oman concept notes. Only after those gates are cleared should a JV term sheet, partner shortlist and financial model be circulated.
