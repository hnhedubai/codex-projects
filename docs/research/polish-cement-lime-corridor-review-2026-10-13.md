# Polish cement and lime review

Date: 2026-10-13  
Scope: Polish industrial-zone catalogue, planned hydrogen plant corridor, approximately 250 km from the Polish-German border.

## Result

The current application catalogue contains 24 Polish zones. None of the seeded Polish zone occupant lists currently contains a confirmed cement or lime producer. The catalogue therefore under-represents this target sector.

## Not counted as a confirmed cement/lime offtaker

Nordkalk Miedzianka is a real limestone quarry and production site near Kielce. The official description supports limestone and aggregates, but does not by itself establish a lime kiln, fossil-fuel process-heat load, or hydrogen offtake requirement. Keep it as a separate raw-material / processing lead, not as a confirmed lime offtaker.

## Validation required before app insertion

1. Geocode each exact plant address.
2. Measure the nearest distance to the authoritative Polish-German border geometry used by the application.
3. Assign the site to the nearest existing zone only when the physical site is inside the zone; do not merge sites merely because they share an operator or corporate group.
4. Add evidence separately for current fuel, annual fuel use, EU ETS status, hydrogen-ready equipment, fuel-switch trials, and estimated hydrogen demand. The sources above establish site identity and industrial activity, not confirmed commercial hydrogen demand.
