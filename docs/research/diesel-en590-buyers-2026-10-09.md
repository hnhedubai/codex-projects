# Diesel EN 590 buyer research — 2026-10-09

**Status:** research findings only; no Accounts, Contacts, roles, or projects
were inserted or changed

## Research question

Find additional credible buyers for Diesel EN 590 / automotive diesel, with a
current procurement signal rather than a historical shipment or supplier
advertisement.

## Qualification rule

A lead is included only when the evidence shows all or most of the following:

- a named buyer or public contracting authority;
- a live or future procurement notice as of 2026-10-09;
- diesel, gas oil, bulk fuel, or CPV 09134220/09134200 demand;
- a dated deadline, contract period, or identifiable procurement route; and
- a buyer-side contact or official tender platform where available.

An open tender proves procurement demand. It does **not** prove that the
buyer will accept an unsolicited FOB/CIF offer, that the buyer is looking for
an overseas principal supplier, or that the buyer has authority to sign a
private offtake agreement outside its stated procurement process.

## Existing or non-new signals not repeated

- Bodø Kommune is already a known CRM buyer; its new 2026 bulk duty-exempt
  diesel framework closes 09 Nov 2026 and includes Iris Service AS and Iris
  Produksjon AS. It is a useful validation signal, not a new account for this
  pass: [TED notice 691230-2026](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/691230-2026).
- Translink has a strong EN 590 demand record, but its 2026–2030 liquid-fuel
  contract is already awarded. It should be monitored for the next cycle, not
  treated as an open bid today: [Find a Tender notice 009157-2026](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/009157-2026/PDF).
- Closed or awarded notices, supplier advertisements, shipment directories,
  tender aggregators without a buyer-side record, and HVO-only opportunities
  were excluded from the reliable-buyer list.

## Recommended CRM treatment

Do not label these organisations as “confirmed buyers” merely from the search.
If approved for insertion, create an account/company observation with:

- role: `Buyer`;
- type: `Government authority` / `SOE` only when the legal form supports it;
- sector: public transport, municipal services, water/wastewater, waste
  management, hospital/public health, or local government as applicable;
- source URL and notice number;
- demand status: `Open tender`;
- product: `Diesel EN 590` (do not infer 10 ppm unless the specification says
  so);
- procurement contact and deadline;
- evidence excerpt and a note that buyer qualification still requires tender
  document review.

The next verification pass should read the official tender documents for
quality/specification, delivery basis, minimum volumes, bidder eligibility,
and whether a non-local supplier can participate. Only after that review
should a candidate be used for a direct commercial approach.
