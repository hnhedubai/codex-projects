# Hydrogen Offtaker Finder — industrial-zone research

**Research date:** 2026-10-13  
**Geography rule:** Poland and Germany, within approximately 250 km of the Polish–German border, on either side.  
**Commercial scope:** industrial energy users that might consume supplied green hydrogen. Industrial parks, zone operators, hydrogen producers, infrastructure developers and associations are discovery sources or enablers—not offtakers by themselves.

## Important interpretation

This is a zone and occupant discovery pass. A listed occupant is **not** a confirmed hydrogen buyer. No hydrogen volume, purity, pressure, delivery route, fuel consumption or buying intent is confirmed unless a separate source proves it. The next step for each occupant is site-level verification against official company, permit, EU ETS, decarbonisation or fuel-switch evidence.

## What is verified versus still open

**Verified at zone/occupant level:** the linked official sources identify the industrial locations, site operators or named occupants and their broad sectors.

**Not verified by this pass:** hydrogen demand, external purchasing intent, 2–10 tonnes/day fit, current fuel quantity, purity, pressure, delivery form, start date, buying department, EU ETS status for each specific installation, or a 24-month fuel-switch signal.

**GIS status:** these are discovery zones, not final 250 km validations. The application should geocode each actual plant/site address and calculate straight-line distance to the authoritative Polish–German border geometry. A zone centroid or city name must not be used as final proof.

## Recommended application treatment

- Store the zone as a `discovery_area` or research container, not as a candidate.
- Store each named occupant as a separate candidate only when its actual plant address is identified.
- Preserve the zone source as provenance on the occupant record.
- Set classification initially to `Possible candidate` or `Needs source verification`.
- Keep hydrogen requirement estimate empty until an evidence-backed basis exists.
- Reject the zone operator, economic-development agency, association, pipeline developer and service provider as offtakers.
- Require a plant-level official or regulatory source before `Confirmed industrial consumer`.
