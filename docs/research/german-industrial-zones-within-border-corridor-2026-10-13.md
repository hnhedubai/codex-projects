# Additional German industrial zones for the Hydrogen Offtaker Finder

**Research date:** 2026-10-13  
**Scope:** German industrial, chemical, process and technology locations within approximately 250 km of the Polish–German border.  
**GIS method:** representative coordinates were geocoded and measured against the application’s GISCO 2024 Polish–German border geometry. Exact plant coordinates remain required.

## Existing German zones retained

The original German list remains in the application: Schwedt, Guben Süd, Bitterfeld-Wolfen, Leuna, ValuePark Schkopau/Böhlen, Premnitz, Piesteritz and Schwarze Pumpe. The Search Workspace now contains 17 German discovery entries in total.

## Qualification warning

These are discovery containers. A park operator, future development company, technology centre, logistics firm, hydrogen producer or infrastructure provider is not automatically an offtaker. Candidate promotion still requires an identifiable industrial occupant, exact plant coordinates and credible evidence of process heat, fossil-fuel replacement or existing hydrogen use.
