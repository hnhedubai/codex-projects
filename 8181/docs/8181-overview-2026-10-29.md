# Opportunity Platform — localhost:8181

**Overview date:** 29 October 2026  
**Address:** http://localhost:8181

The Opportunity Platform is a local workspace for researching energy projects, investors, potential customers and other commercial counterparties. It brings research, contact information, evidence, diligence and outreach workflows together in one interface.

This overview describes the local application and its source, not a verification of every record. The server responded on 29 October 2026.

## Workspaces

### Listed Projects

[Open Listed Projects](http://localhost:8181/listed-projects)

Selected projects for investment review, with information such as project owner, country, technology, capacity, stage, indicative CAPEX, generation, offtake, financing and bankability. Supporting documents and outstanding requirements can be reviewed alongside each record.

The module documentation describes 11 user-supplied projects, including five Croatian portfolio projects. Source-reported ownership, financial projections, offtake and financing are not automatically independently verified. Cernik is documented as on hold.

### Wind Projects

[Open Wind Projects](http://localhost:8181/wind-projects)

A research pipeline of wind projects and developers. It records location, reported scale, partnership signals, opportunity status, sources, contacts, diligence notes and outreach activity. A project listing does not itself confirm that an investment opportunity is currently open.

### Investors & JV

[Open Investors & JV](http://localhost:8181/investors-jv)

Research on potential investors, developers and joint-venture partners, including geography, sector, investment interests, preferred scale, contacts, evidence and relationship status. The outreach workflow can prepare an anonymous project pitch, select a project, retain draft versions and record offer history. JEV assessment controls are available; an assessment is not confirmation of a current investment mandate.

### Hydrogen Offtakers

[Open Hydrogen Offtakers](http://localhost:8181/hydrogen-offtakers)

A company pipeline for potential industrial hydrogen customers, including industry, geography, reported demand signals, offtake status and supporting evidence. A research signal is not a confirmed purchase requirement or signed offtake agreement.

## Common tools

- Browse portfolios and open individual project or company records.
- Search and filter records, including country inclusion or exclusion.
- Adjust table column order, width and visibility, and navigate pages of records.
- Keep research notes, sources, diligence information and relationship progress.
- Maintain contacts and, when configured, use Reoon to check email addresses. Saving a contact with an email can trigger a paid check; mailbox verification does not by itself prove the named person owns the address.
- Prepare and save outreach drafts, record follow-up dates and use the configured Pulse integration for supported modules. Sending depends on service configuration and recipient checks; follow-up dates are reminders in the workspace, not automatic notifications.
- Export supported workspace information and use the documented checkpoint process for local application files. Checkpoints do not automatically include browser localStorage or credentials.

## Storage and access

The application is local-first. Some source records and operational history are stored in the 8181 application, while drafts, workflow notes, selections and table settings may be stored in the current browser. Reoon results and per-module Pulse send histories have local server-side stores.

Browser data belongs to a particular browser profile and origin. Use `http://localhost:8181` consistently: `localhost:8181` and `127.0.0.1:8181` have separate browser storage. Clearing browser storage can remove locally saved drafts and preferences. Response status may be recorded manually; the reviewed 8181 follow-up investigation does not establish automatic inbox reply tracking.

The application folder is `C:\Users\mohdz\codex-projects\8181`. Its documented start command is `npm start`. Backups and exports can contain private contacts and commercial information and should be handled accordingly.

## How to use the information

The platform organises research and outreach. A record can contain historical, incomplete or user-reported information. Confirm project ownership, current investor interest, contact identity, financing, offtake and other material claims with up-to-date evidence before relying on them commercially.
