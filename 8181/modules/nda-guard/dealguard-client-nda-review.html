MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Agreement date: Pending client approval · Version 1.3 · English

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made between the parties identified below to protect confidential information and preserve each party’s exclusive relationships while they explore potential business relationships, collaborations, or transactions.

Party A: [PARTY A NAME]
Party B: [PARTY B NAME]

1. Definition of Confidential Information

“Confidential Information” means information disclosed by either Party to the other, whether orally, visually, in writing, electronically, by email, instant message, or by signed Annexure, that is designated confidential or should reasonably be understood to be confidential.

This includes financial information, business plans, proposals, contracts, market analyses, technical information, proprietary software, data, tools, designs, methodologies, databases, spreadsheets, presentations, emails, and other non-public information providing a competitive advantage.

2. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party shall hold Confidential Information in strict confidence, protect it with reasonable care, use it only for the stated Purpose, and limit access to employees, agents, advisers, and consultants who need to know and are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations.

The Receiving Party shall maintain reasonable electronic security, promptly report any suspected or actual data breach, and return or destroy Confidential Information upon written request or termination, subject to lawful retention requirements.

3. Non-Circumvention

A Protected Relationship means any client, counterparty, investor, project, transaction, or opportunity identified in a signed Annexure or identified in writing as “Protected” by email, WhatsApp, or another written communication. Protection begins on disclosure.

Once a client and project have been disclosed, the Receiving Party shall not independently pursue, solicit, negotiate with, provide services to, transact with, or enter into an arrangement with that client concerning the disclosed project, even if the Receiving Party already knew or had an existing relationship with the client.

Any unsolicited direct or indirect contact concerning the disclosed project must be referred to and handled only through the Introducing Party. The Receiving Party may not respond substantively, negotiate, provide services, or conclude an arrangement without prior written consent.

The restriction includes action through an affiliate, related company, employee, agent, adviser, consultant, broker, nominee, intermediary, or other person or entity acting for, controlled by, or connected with the Receiving Party. It continues for five years from disclosure.

If a breach results in a closed deal, commission is based on the final closed deal size and the applicable project Commission Schedule. In a breach, earned commission is divided equally between the parties and the breaching party pays reasonable documented legal charges and breach-related costs.

4. Exclusions

Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information the Receiving Party can prove through reliable written records:

  • was publicly available before disclosure or becomes public without the Receiving Party’s act, omission, misuse, disclosure, negligence, or security failure;
  • was lawfully known before disclosure;
  • was lawfully received from a third party entitled to disclose it without breach of confidentiality;
  • was independently developed without using or referring to Confidential Information; or
  • must be disclosed under law, court order, regulatory requirement, or government authority.

Where legally permitted, the Receiving Party will give advance notice, disclose only the minimum required, cooperate with protective measures, and notify the Disclosing Party afterward if advance notice was prohibited. These exclusions do not override non-circumvention protection.

5. Term and Termination

The Agreement remains in force for five years. Either Party may terminate it with 30 days’ written notice; termination stops future disclosures but does not affect obligations relating to prior disclosures.

Immediate termination is permitted for unauthorized disclosure or use, circumvention, failure to report a data breach, failure to return or destroy information, or repeated breach after written warning. Confidentiality and non-circumvention continue for five years from each disclosure. Trade-secret information remains protected while it qualifies as a trade secret.

6. Remedies

Unauthorized disclosure, misuse, circumvention, or attempted circumvention may cause irreparable harm. The non-breaching Party may seek injunctive relief, specific performance, and other lawful remedies.

The breaching Party is responsible for reasonable documented losses, legal charges, investigation expenses, enforcement costs, and other costs directly caused by the breach. If a breach results in a closed deal, earned commission is divided equally between the parties under the applicable Commission Schedule.

7. Notices

Formal notices must be in writing and sent to the official email addresses stated in the Agreement or later notified in writing. An email notice is effective when sent unless the sender receives a delivery-failure message. If delivery fails, notice may be sent by courier or registered mail.

WhatsApp, portal messages, and informal communications may record disclosures or project details but do not constitute formal legal notices.

8. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates. The Parties will first attempt good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within 30 days after written notice, the dispute may be referred to arbitration administered by the Dubai International Arbitration Centre under its rules in force when arbitration begins.

The tribunal will consist of one arbitrator. The legal seat is the DIFC, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. The arbitration language is English and the award is final and binding. Either Party may seek urgent or interim relief from the competent DIFC courts.

9. General Terms

This Agreement, signed Annexures, and Commission Schedules form the entire agreement regarding confidentiality and non-circumvention, while preserving prior disclosures and agreed commission terms unless expressly replaced in writing.

Main Agreement amendments require a written document signed by both Parties. New Annexures and Commission Schedules may be approved electronically. Delay in enforcement is not a waiver. Invalid provisions do not affect the remainder. Assignment requires prior written consent, except for a merger, restructuring, or sale of substantially all business or assets.

The Agreement may be signed electronically or through the DealGuard digital-signature workflow. Electronic signatures and executed copies are treated as originals to the extent permitted by applicable law.

10. Annexures and Commission Schedules

Specific projects, clients, transactions, or opportunities may be recorded in an Annexure. The Annexure may include the project name, Protected Relationship, disclosure date, commission rate or amount, final closed deal-size basis, payer, currency, payment trigger, and equal-split terms in the event of breach.

An Annexure or Commission Schedule becomes part of this Agreement when approved electronically by both Parties, and applies to the identified client/project unless it expressly varies a stated provision.
